# Category groups Articles only, and is fully localised

Category arrived from Strapi's LaunchPad demo carrying two jobs: a `manyToMany` relation to Article that behaves like a taxonomy, and a `manyToOne` relation to Product that behaves like ownership. We keep the first, drop the second, and localise the surviving relation properly. Category groups Articles and nothing else; Products stay organised by `Featured`.

The Product side modelled nothing. Every product-side category was a fragment of its own Product's name — `Content Rocket Booster` produced `rocket` and `booster`, `Enterprise Integration Kit` produced `integration` — so `manyToOne` was not a mis-shaped taxonomy awaiting correction but a faithful encoding of what the seeder did. A term derived from one Product's name can by construction never apply to a second, so nothing was ever grouped. A Product's capabilities already have a home in `shared.perks`, rendered as bullets twenty lines above the category pills on the same page.

The localisation defect was in the schema, not the data. `article.categories` carries no `pluginOptions.i18n` block, while every other field on Article explicitly sets `localized: true`. In Strapi a relation without that setting is not localised, so its value is shared across a document's locales — the French localisation inherited the English article's category links verbatim. The result was visible on a published page: FR article #7 rendered `dashboard · content · analyses · contenu · tableau de bord · marketing`, six pills with two concepts appearing twice in two languages. The relation gains `i18n.localized: true`.

## Considered Options

**Keeping a product taxonomy, correctly shaped.** Reshaping `category.product` to `manyToMany` would have made grouping possible rather than merely encoded. We rejected it because no Product grouping exists to serve: the product listing organises by `Featured` alone, and the glossary has no word for a group of Products. adu-dev is template-first and later a live site selling AI-assisted development services, so the need may become real — but it cannot be named well before the services exist, and shipping an empty concept means every clone inherits it. The trigger for revisiting is stated under Consequences.

**A non-localised taxonomy.** Dropping i18n from Category entirely would make cross-locale duplication impossible by construction and leave editors one list to maintain, at the cost of French readers seeing `dashboard` and `content`. Of the six surviving terms only three genuinely translate (`analytics`/`analyses`, `content`/`contenu`, `dashboard`/`tableau de bord`), one differs by an accent and two are identical, so the value of translation here is thinner than it first appears. We rejected it anyway: this repository's live site is English-only, so neither model costs its owner anything, which makes the decision a question of what a clone that genuinely translates needs — and a template whose claim is that i18n works cannot ship a French page rendering English pills. A third shape, non-localised identity carrying a translated label, is not available: Strapi's i18n is per-entry, so a field may opt out of localisation on a localised type but a non-localised type cannot have a localised field.

**Renaming Category to Topic.** "Topic" is editorial by construction — nobody attaches a Topic to a Product — so the name itself would resist the drift that produced this decision. We declined it because the drift was structural rather than lexical, and dropping the `product` relation removes the cause. The cost was measured rather than assumed: roughly thirteen source files, the public `/api/categories` path, and a regenerated seed export, cheapest bundled into the follow-up work that must re-export the seed anyway, and materially more expensive after the first real article or the first clone. `_Avoid_` in `CONTEXT.md` does the guarding instead.

**A stable slug, and draft/publish.** Both were considered and deliberately deferred. Category URLs and category filtering are out of scope, so a slug would be identity that nothing reads, and a taxonomy of nine names has nothing to draft.

## Consequences

`article.categories` stays `manyToMany` with its `category.articles` inverse and gains `i18n.localized: true`. `category.product` and `product.categories` are removed. The inverse has no reader once the populate chain is trimmed, but it is what any future category listing or filter would traverse, and it costs nothing to keep.

Under full localisation the taxonomy must be translated before the content that uses it, or a translator opening a new localised Article gets an empty category picker — which is exactly what happened when the French articles were authored in September 2024, eighteen months before French categories existed. This is accepted rather than mitigated. The admin already prevents the wrong choice: `@strapi/content-manager` filters relation candidates to the source's locale when the target is localised, so the failure mode is a visible empty list rather than a bad link.

`CONTEXT.md` gains an Editorial section defining Article and Category. Article earns an entry on the glossary's own test — the content type is `article` while the frontend calls it a blog post — and a Category entry referring to an undefined Article would be incoherent.

Revisit this record when real services exist and what groups them is known; that grouping should be a concept named for services, not this one widened again. Revisit the slug when the first category URL or filter is built.
